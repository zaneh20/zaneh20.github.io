ArcLatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

ArcLatch is an arcade game developed by arklabs. This policy explains how ArcLatch and the Google services included in it handle information.

Information stored on your device

ArcLatch stores gameplay and preference information locally in app-private storage: overall and per-map best scores, selected world, music and haptic settings, a cached supporter entitlement, and achievement unlocks, counters and progress. Achievement recognition points are separate from competitive leaderboard scores. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy these preferences through your Google-configured platform backup service. arklabs does not operate an ArcLatch account system, analytics server, cloud-save service, or custom backend.

Tutorial and community links

The in-game tutorial video is bundled with ArcLatch and plays locally. Watching it does not require YouTube, an internet connection or a separate sign-in.

Credits includes an optional invitation to the Arklabs Discord community. Opening it hands the invitation URL to Discord or your browser; it does not automatically join a server. ArcLatch does not include an in-game chat service or request your Discord credentials. Activity on Discord is governed by its own terms, privacy practices and applicable age requirements.

Advertising and consent

ArcLatch contains Google Mobile Ads for an optional rewarded continue and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent choices. There are no banner, interstitial, startup, app-open, or automatic ads. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID. These data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. UMP processes consent status and presents privacy choices where required.

You can play ArcLatch without choosing to watch a rewarded ad. Availability and personalization of advertising depend on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's services.

Google Play Billing

ArcLatch offers an optional, non-consumable supporter_all_maps purchase. Google Play processes the transaction and localized price. ArcLatch receives product and purchase state, acknowledges completed purchases, restores owned entitlement, and stores a local entitlement cache. arklabs does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Purchase information may be processed by Google for transaction delivery, restoration, accounting, security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Google Play Games and Rankings

ArcLatch offers optional online Rankings through Google Play Games Services. Ordinary gameplay and existing local progress remain available without connecting a Play Games profile. When connected, ArcLatch uses your Google-managed gamer identity and submits completed run scores to the selected map's leaderboard. Rankings may display gamer display names, avatars, scores and ranks according to Google Play Games profile visibility settings. ArcLatch does not request your Google email address, access tokens for a developer server, or friends list, and does not operate its own player account service.

Google's Play Games SDK processes gamer identity, gameplay/service data such as leaderboard scores, and SDK analytics and diagnostics to provide and improve its services. Google encrypts this service data in transit. ArcLatch stores new per-map records locally. A bounded, app-private queue may retain a completed score and its originating gamer ID for a later submission; this queue is not backed up and is removed when submission is acknowledged. Local records can be removed through Android app storage controls. Google-held game data and profile visibility can be managed or deleted through Google Play Games profile or Google-account controls; uninstalling ArcLatch does not itself delete Google's leaderboard records.

ArcLatch includes local achievements and optional Google Play Games achievements. When you connect a Play Games profile, the game reads that player's online achievement progress and sends earned unlocks or incremental progress to Google Play Games Services. Local progress remains available offline; eligible pending progress can synchronise when the service becomes available. ArcLatch checks the connected player before syncing and may ask you to confirm the profile for existing local progress. It stores Play Games player IDs and sync bookkeeping locally to keep achievement progress associated with the correct profile. Google manages online achievement records under its service and privacy settings. ArcLatch does not request or store your Google password. You can manage or delete Google-held game data through Google Play Games or Google-account controls.

Purposes and recipients

Information is processed to provide app functionality, consent choices, optional advertising, purchase and entitlement functionality, diagnostics/analytics performed by the advertising SDK, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Relevant information is handled by Google through Google Mobile Ads, UMP, Google Play, Android backup, and associated service providers under Google's terms and privacy practices. ArcLatch does not sell data through an arklabs-operated data service.

Security and retention

ArcLatch uses Android app-private storage and supported Google SDK transport. Local preferences remain until you clear ArcLatch storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup/restore settings. Advertising, consent, diagnostic, and purchase records controlled by Google are retained according to Google's applicable service policies, legal obligations, and user controls.

Deletion and choices

You can delete ArcLatch's local gameplay and preference data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. You can change available Google consent/privacy choices from ArcLatch when UMP requires that option and through applicable Google/device controls. Google Play purchase history and restored ownership are controlled through Google Play and Google-account controls; uninstalling ArcLatch does not cancel or erase a completed non-consumable purchase.

Children and target audience

ArcLatch is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not designed or directed as a children's/Families app. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing in your country, use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ArcLatch features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.
