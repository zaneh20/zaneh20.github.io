ArcLatch Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

ArcLatch is an arcade game developed by arklabs. This policy explains how ArcLatch and the Google services included in it handle information.

Information stored on your device

ArcLatch stores gameplay and preference information locally in app-private storage: best score, selected world, music and haptic settings, and a cached supporter entitlement. Android backup or device-transfer features may copy these preferences through your Google-configured platform backup service. arklabs does not operate an ArcLatch account system, analytics server, cloud-save service, or custom backend.

Advertising and consent

ArcLatch contains Google Mobile Ads for an optional rewarded continue and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for consent choices. There are no banner, interstitial, startup, app-open, or automatic ads. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers such as the advertising ID or app set ID. These data may be used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. UMP processes consent status and presents privacy choices where required.

You can play ArcLatch without choosing to watch a rewarded ad. Availability and personalization of advertising depend on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's services.

Google Play Billing

ArcLatch offers an optional, non-consumable supporter_all_maps purchase. Google Play processes the transaction and localized price. ArcLatch receives product and purchase state, acknowledges completed purchases, restores owned entitlement, and stores a local entitlement cache. arklabs does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Purchase information may be processed by Google for transaction delivery, restoration, accounting, security, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Purposes and recipients

Information is processed to provide app functionality, consent choices, optional advertising, purchase and entitlement functionality, diagnostics/analytics performed by the advertising SDK, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance. Relevant information is handled by Google through Google Mobile Ads, UMP, Google Play, Android backup, and associated service providers under Google's terms and privacy practices. ArcLatch does not sell data through an arklabs-operated data service.

Security and retention

ArcLatch uses Android app-private storage and supported Google SDK transport. Local preferences remain until you clear ArcLatch storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup/restore settings. Advertising, consent, diagnostic, and purchase records controlled by Google are retained according to Google's applicable service policies, legal obligations, and user controls.

Deletion and choices

You can delete ArcLatch's local gameplay and preference data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. You can change available Google consent/privacy choices from ArcLatch when UMP requires that option and through applicable Google/device controls. Google Play purchase history and restored ownership are controlled through Google Play and Google-account controls; uninstalling ArcLatch does not cancel or erase a completed non-consumable purchase.

Children and target audience

ArcLatch is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not designed or directed as a children's/Families app. If you are under the age required to consent to data processing in your country, use the app only with the involvement of a parent or guardian where required.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ArcLatch features, SDKs, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when a revised policy is published.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact arklabs using the developer contact email displayed on ArcLatch's Google Play store listing.
